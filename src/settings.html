Configure your voice dictation settings

Accessibility Permission Required

To type text at your cursor, Whisp needs Accessibility permission:

  1. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility
  2. Click the + button and add Whisp
  3. Enable the toggle next to Whisp

Get your API key from platform.openai.com/api-keys
